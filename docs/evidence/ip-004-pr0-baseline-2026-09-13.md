## Clotho semantic-E2E baseline

Read operations are available: World, Canon and bounded Event search returned the
served revision and World-owned Event IDs. For example, searching `조선` returned
`조선 건국·태조 즉위` (`…0104`) and `국호 조선 사용` (`…0105`) with their Canon
memberships.

The connected Clotho contract exposes `change.validate` and `change.commit`, but
PR-0 did not write to the shared public dogfood World. PR-B must use a dedicated,
resettable synthetic World and verify coarse creation plus at least three
progressive refinements through Publication and Atropos readback. A successful
HTTP response or row count is explicitly insufficient evidence.

## Boundaries reaffirmed

- IP-003's World-owned Event/Relation identity and Canon N:M membership remain
  fixed; a UI or authoring shortcut must not duplicate identity by Canon.
- Narrative remains authored prose in a single Canon context; it is not a derived
  merge target.
- Publication is rebuildable and revision-addressed; canonical PostgreSQL remains
  durable source of truth.
- M5 lifecycle, portability, governance/access and operations are out of scope.
